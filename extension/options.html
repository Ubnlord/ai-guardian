AI Guardian — extension

Point the extension at your Guardian instance and paste an API key.

How to use it

  1. Click the Guardian icon in the toolbar, or press Alt+Shift+G.
  2. Or right-click anywhere on a page → Check this page with AI Guardian.
  3. Select text first and the selection is checked instead of the whole page.

The extension reads only the page address, its title, and any text you selected. It never reads passwords, form fields, or your browsing history.
